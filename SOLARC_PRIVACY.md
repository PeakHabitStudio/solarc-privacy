# SolArc Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

SolArc is an illustrative sun, sunscreen and skin model published by Peak Habit Studio. It does not measure ultraviolet radiation, sunscreen protection or skin, and it does not provide medical advice.

## Information stored on your device

SolArc can save settings you choose, such as a city, skin type, sunscreen settings and surroundings, along with your in-app sun exposure and sunscreen application history. This information is stored in the app on your device so the model and reminder can work. SolArc does not request precise device location.

## Information sent to us

SolArc has no account system or advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. Its Android and Wear OS apps do not request internet access. The Windows and Zepp apps also process the model locally. SolArc does not send your settings, location or history to Peak Habit Studio or another service. Reminder scheduling and notifications are handled on your device. Stores and operating systems may independently process purchase, installation or diagnostic information under their own privacy policies.

## Your choices and retention

Your saved app information remains in the app's local storage until you reset the app's day history, clear the app's storage or uninstall SolArc. Cloud backup is disabled for the Android and Wear OS apps. Device-to-device transfer behavior can still depend on your device manufacturer and operating-system version. You do not need to contact us to delete information stored only on your device.

## Children

SolArc is a general utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. Questions about privacy can be sent to support@peakhabit.com.
